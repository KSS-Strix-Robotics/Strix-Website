About STRIX

The KSS robotics club is a group of passionate young minds who believe in the importance of investing in the innovators of tomorrow: students. We do this through STEM education. In our club, we allow students to pursue STEM not only in a collaborative environment, but in a professional one as well. This means students cultivate a sense of business while developing a variety of valuable skills, ranging from reliability, project management, and social networking, to programming, mechanics, and design-based thinking. However, we can only contribute in this way largely because of the competitions the club takes part in. Our main one is the FIRST Robotics Competition. Our team is as excited as ever to seize the opportunity and compete in this amazing competition like we did the year prior!

About Our Team

Our team is passionate about robotics and we've been diligently working to develop our skills and knowledge in this field. This year we are planning on competing in the 2026 FIRST Robotics Competition that is taking place in Vancouver. This competition involves building a robot that has to complete several tasks. These tasks are not known to us yet as the release date is January 10th. We will have 6 weeks to complete this robot and the competition will take place in early March.
